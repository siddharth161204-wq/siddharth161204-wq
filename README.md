# Siddharth Satte

Independent researcher, Pune, India. Thermal and lifetime models of electrochemical energy systems, and what the aggregate costs.

Most thermal and lifetime models rest on an aggregate: a cell as a uniform heat source, a stack as its mean cell voltage. My work measures what that assumption costs, how large the error is, what sets it, and how to carry it to a decision rather than assume it away. Every study here was run the same way: predictions committed to the repository before the analysis, failed predictions reported beside the held ones, and code, results and a one-page note archived with a DOI.

## Manuscripts (sole author)

- **Error of the uniform heat-generation assumption in base-cooled prismatic lithium-ion cells: the roles of current collection and event duration.** A 70 Ah prismatic cell modelled from published parameters, with the heat distributed over the foils, tab strips and foil-to-terminal joints by a current-collector solve; the uniform source underestimates the steady peak surface temperature by 0.06 K for a welded roll and by up to 5.9 K for the narrowest tabs, and misplaces the hot spot. Submitted for publication, October 2026. Preprint: [doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7570666](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7570666). Code and data: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23053419](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23053419).
- **Worst-cell degradation and stack-mean life projections in PEM fuel cell stacks: the IEEE PHM 2014 benchmark and a test on a heavy-duty stack.** The worst cell lost voltage 16.7 and 21.4 % faster than the stack mean, against 7.5 and 20.0 % from selection alone. Under review. Preprint: [doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7564865](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7564865). Code: [doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23054091](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23054091).

## Five pre-registered studies

| Repository | What it measures | Archive |
|---|---|---|
| [geslin-floor-pilot](https://github.com/siddharth161204-wq/geslin-floor-pilot) | The cell-to-cell lifetime floor in a public 92-cell dataset: duplicates under one protocol differ by 3.0 % on average and 8.7 % at the 95th percentile. | [10.5281/zenodo.23205412](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23205412) |
| [thermal-lever-duplicate-floor](https://github.com/siddharth161204-wq/thermal-lever-duplicate-floor) | Lifetime against temperature, measured in two laboratories' data (144 KIT cells, 86 Sandia cells): minus 3.45 to plus 1.91 % per kelvin near 35 °C with the sign set by the charge rate, and minus 2.44 for the same cell at Sandia; negative in every KIT protocol from 10 to 25 °C. Run through an eight-cell module, those levers pass the measured floor only in cold air at 1C and above; the experiment that would settle it needs 3 duplicate pairs held 5 K apart in a 10 °C chamber or 25 pairs held 2 K apart at 35 °C. Within the datasets' own replicate sets the lever is not recoverable (recovery coefficient −0.09, −2.05 to +1.37, over 106 KIT pairs): existing replicates cannot measure the thermal share. | [10.5281/zenodo.23309130](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23309130) |
| [m50-two-cell-pilot](https://github.com/siddharth161204-wq/m50-two-cell-pilot) | A neighbour cell solved as one temperature and as a resolved field: the resolved hottest point reaches the self-heating temperature about a minute before the lumped node; the busbars carry 86 to 90 % of its heat intake. | [10.5281/zenodo.23253737](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23253737) |
| [m50-neighbour-onset-kinetics](https://github.com/siddharth161204-wq/m50-neighbour-onset-kinetics) | Published Bayesian runaway kinetics on that neighbour: over 200 posterior draws the lumped node never starts first, and the lead runs from 15 to 59 s. Against sixteen measured two-cell propagation tests, scored test by test, the resolved hot spot predicts which tests propagate in 11 of 16 but 130 to 320 s early, the whole-cell clock predicts when but rarely whether, and no ignition condition reproduces outcomes that do not follow the trigger's peak. Asked the other way round, the delivered fraction of the trigger's ejected energy that each test would need overlaps between the tests that propagated (0 to 0.09) and those that did not (0 to 0.16): no single fraction reproduces the sixteen outcomes, and propagation at a given trigger reads as a probability. Read from each test's own time, the whole-cell clock near its threshold reproduces six of the nine measured propagation times within 10 s at 0.2 to 4.7 % of the ejected energy, while the hot spot reproduces none. | [10.5281/zenodo.23279455](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23279455) |
| [m50-radial-vs-resolved-field](https://github.com/siddharth161204-wq/m50-radial-vs-resolved-field) | The radial thermal model behind a published multi-unit cell model, tested in three dimensions: within 0.29 K with insulated ends; a one-term end correction cuts the cooled-end offset from 1.19 to 0.10 K, where the same group's learned-error core estimator leaves 1.14 of a 1.18 K miss at its registered noise level; carried to the current each unit draws, the radial field overstates the unit-to-unit spread by 36 % (M50) and 95 % (4680), which the end correction brings to 5 and 3 %. | [10.5281/zenodo.23320969](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23320969) |

Across the five studies, 145 predictions were committed before the data were opened; 57 failed and 2 could not be scored, and all are reported with the rest.
